## Favorite Existing Apps

### Pinterest
- **Category:** Lifestyle / Inspiration  
- **Mobile:** Mobile-first, swipe-based design makes the app easy to browse anywhere.  
- **Story:** Helps people discover creative ideas and save inspiration boards.  
- **Market:** Anyone who enjoys DIY, decor, recipes, fashion, or project planning.  
- **Habit:** Highly habitual — users scroll daily for new ideas and save pins based on interests.  
- **Scope:** Broad — supports images, videos, shopping links, boards, and personalized feeds.

### TikTok
- **Category:** Entertainment / Social  
- **Mobile:** Primarily mobile — uses camera, gestures, and vertical scrolling.  
- **Story:** Users discover short-form videos tailored to their interests.  
- **Market:** Teens to adults who enjoy short video content.  
- **Habit:** Extremely habit forming; users check it multiple times per day.  
- **Scope:** Broad — includes posting, editing, messaging, sound library, and live video.

### PlantIn
- **Category:** Education / Productivity  
- **Mobile:** Uses camera for plant scanning and notifications for reminders.  
- **Story:** Helps users care for plants through watering schedules and plant guides.  
- **Market:** Plant owners, new gardeners, hobbyists, and students.  
- **Habit:** Used weekly or daily depending on user’s plant care routine.  
- **Scope:** Medium — plant scanning, plant database, reminders, and tips.

## New App Ideas

### GardenLover
- **Description:** Helps users know whether a plant can grow in their region (USDA zone), shows best planting seasons, gives weather-based watering reminders, and tracks plants in a personal garden log.  
- **Category:** Education / Productivity  
- **Mobile:** Requires GPS, notifications, and optional camera features.  
- **Story:** Helps users confidently choose plants that thrive in their climate.  
- **Market:** Gardeners, plant lovers, homeowners, beginners, and students.  
- **Habit:** Used weekly or daily depending on plant care needs.  
- **Scope:**  
  - **V1:** Plant suitability checker, seasonal guide, watering suggestions, My Garden list  
  - **V2:** Add plant photos, growth timeline  
  - **V3:** AI plant recognition, disease detection, advanced forecasting

### Weather Outfit Picker
- **Description:** Suggests daily outfits based on temperature, humidity, and user style preferences.  
- **Category:** Lifestyle / Utility  
- **Mobile:** Uses live weather data and notifications.  
- **Story:** Helps users dress appropriately for the day.  
- **Market:** Teens, adults, students, and busy professionals.  
- **Habit:** Daily use.  
- **Scope:**  
  - V1: Outfit suggestions  
  - V2: Closet tracking  
  - V3: Social outfit sharing  

### Meal Prep Planner
- **Description:** Generates weekly meal plans based on diet preferences and budget.  
- **Category:** Food / Productivity  
- **Mobile:** Helps with grocery lists, quick recipes, and weekly plans.  
- **Story:** Makes meal planning easier and healthier.  
- **Market:** College students, families, fitness users.  
- **Habit:** Weekly use.  
- **Scope:**  
  - V1: Meal plan + grocery list  
  - V2: Nutrition tracking  
  - V3: Community recipes
